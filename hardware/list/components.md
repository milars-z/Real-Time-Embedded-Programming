# Hardware Components List

## Main Hardware

- Main Controller: Raspberry Pi 5 (4GB) 
- Camera Module: Raspberry Pi Camera Module 3
- Microphone: VEETOP USB Microphone
- Speaker: USB Speaker
- Servo Driver: PCA9685
- Servos: MG996R (x3), MG90 (x1)
- Display (Optional): OSOYOO 3.5-inch DSI touchscreen
- Robotic Arm: EEZYbotARM Mk2(3D-printed assembly)
- Power Supply: External 5V Power Supply (for PCA9685)

## 3D Printed Robitc Arm Assembly Parts

The following parts are used for assembling the EEZYbotARM Mk2 robotic arm listed above.
- 1 × M6 self-locking nut
- 1 × M6 × 25 screw
- 2 × M3 self-locking nuts
- 2 × M3 × 20 screws
- 1 × M3 × 10 hex recessed head screw
- 9 × M4 self-locking nuts
- 1 × M4 × 40 screw
- 1 × M4 × 30 screw
- 5 × M4 × 20 screws
- 1 × M4 × 60 mm threaded rod
- 1 × M4 × 32 mm threaded rod
- 25 × 6 mm ball spheres
- 1 × 606zz bearing
- Some M4 washers
